Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Winchester, UK

At Lauryn & Littles, safeguarding the privacy, safety, and confidentiality of families, children, and caregivers is central to our boutique community. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, store, process, and protect personal data in compliance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

♥
Our Commitment to Privacy: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. Information collected is strictly used to facilitate safe, verified childcare introductions, background screening, and booking confirmations.

1. About Us & Data Controller

Lauryn & Littles operates as a boutique childcare introductory platform based in Winchester, Hampshire, United Kingdom. For the purposes of the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, Lauryn & Littles is the Data Controller responsible for your personal information.

2. Personal Data We Collect

We only collect personal data reasonably necessary to coordinate reliable childcare and verify professional standards:

A. From Families & Parents:

  • Contact details: Parent/guardian full name, UK telephone number, and email address.
  • Location details: Residential town/suburb in Winchester, Hampshire, and postcode for booking dispatch.
  • Care requirements: Children’s first names, ages, daily routines, dietary preferences, and emergency contact details.
  • Special health information: Allergies, medical conditions, or routine guidelines willingly provided by parents to ensure child safety during a booking.
  • Access notes: Parking, entry instructions, or pet information.

B. From Sitters & Caregivers:

  • Identity & contact details: Full name, date of birth, residential address, telephone number, email, and photographic ID (e.g. UK Driving Licence or Passport).
  • Vetting credentials: Enhanced DBS (Disclosure and Barring Service) certificate number or DBS Update Service verification status.
  • Experience & references: Childcare resume/CV, previous employment history, professional references, and paediatric first aid certifications.

3. Lawful Basis for Processing

Under the UK GDPR, we process personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Contractual Necessity: To take steps at your request to process quotes, match families with suitable caregivers, and coordinate bookings.
  • Consent: When you willingly submit special category data (such as child medical or allergy notes) or join our caregiver waiting list.
  • Legitimate Interests: To verify caregiver suitability, uphold community safeguarding standards, and provide customer support.
  • Legal Obligation: To comply with relevant UK statutory safeguarding and record-keeping regulations.

4. How We Use Personal Data

We process personal details for legitimate business and child-welfare purposes, including:

  • Assessing family care requests and preparing tailored quotes.
  • Introducing compatible families to vetted, local babysitters and nannies.
  • Conducting personal phone interviews and verifying credentials and photo ID for sitters on our waiting list.
  • Facilitating secure introductory contact between matched parties.
  • Fulfilling child welfare and safeguarding obligations under UK law.

5. Disclosure of Data

We treat your data with strict confidentiality. Personal data is only shared in the following situations:

  • Between Matched Parties: Once a booking is agreed, relevant contact details (parent name, phone, suburb, and sitter name, phone, and confirmation details) are exchanged to enable care.
  • Essential Service Providers: Trusted cloud database, hosting, and messaging providers assisting our operations under confidentiality agreements.
  • Emergency Services: In an acute medical emergency, essential health or location details may be shared with emergency first responders (NHS, Paramedics, or 999).
  • Legal Requirements: Where required by law, court order, or statutory child safeguarding guidelines in England & Wales.

6. Data Security & Storage

We implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Data is stored on secure, encrypted servers.

Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or to satisfy legal, accounting, and safeguarding requirements.

7. Your Legal Rights Under UK GDPR

Under the UK GDPR, you have extensive individual rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right of Access: You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you (Subject Access Request).
  • Right to Rectification: You can request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to Erasure: You can request deletion of your personal records ("Right to be Forgotten"), subject to legal retention duties.
  • Right to Restrict or Object: You can object to or restrict processing under certain circumstances.

To exercise any of these rights, please email us at privacy@laurynandlittles.co.uk. We respond to all verified requests within one calendar month.

8. Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses minimal, essential cookies and analytics to ensure proper functionality and performance. Cookies do not track sensitive child details and can be controlled through your browser settings.
