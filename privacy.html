Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Australia

At Lauryn & Littles, safeguarding the privacy, safety, and confidentiality of families, children, and caregivers is at the very core of our boutique childcare community. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, hold, use, and protect your personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

♥
Our Commitment to Families: We never sell, rent, or trade personal data to third-party marketing companies. Information provided is strictly utilized to facilitate verified childcare introductions, safety screening, and booking confirmations.

1. About Lauryn & Littles

Lauryn & Littles operates as a boutique Australian childcare platform connecting families with independent, thoroughly vetted babysitters and nannies for scheduled and last-minute care needs. For the purposes of the Australian Privacy Principles, Lauryn & Littles is the entity responsible for managing your personal records.

2. Personal Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that is reasonably necessary to coordinate reliable childcare and verify professional standards:

A. From Families & Parents:

  • Contact details: Parent/guardian full name, Australian mobile phone number, and email address.
  • Location details: Residential address, suburb, state/territory (e.g., NSW, VIC, QLD, WA, SA, TAS, ACT, NT), and postcode for booking dispatch.
  • Child information: First names, ages, daily routines, bedtime requirements, dietary preferences, and emergency contact details.
  • Sensitive health information: Allergies (e.g., anaphylaxis, food allergies), medical management plans (e.g., asthma, EpiPen), or special developmental needs provided willingly by parents to ensure child health and safety during a booking.
  • Property/Access instructions: Parking notes, gate codes, pet details, or entry instructions.

B. From Sitters & Caregivers:

  • Identity & contact details: Full legal name, date of birth, residential address, mobile number, photo identification (e.g., Australian Driver Licence or Passport).
  • Vetting & safety credentials: Working with Children Check (WWCC) or state equivalent (such as Queensland Blue Card) registration number, expiry date, and issuing jurisdiction.
  • Qualifications & certifications: HLTAID012 / HLTAID004 First Aid and CPR certificates, early childhood education certificates, National Police Certificate, and professional childcare references.
  • Profile information: Experience history, hourly rate expectations, availability, and caregiver biography.

3. How We Collect Personal Information

We collect personal information directly from you through multiple channels, including:

  • Online quote, enquiry, and registration forms on our website.
  • Direct communication via email, phone, SMS, or messaging platforms.
  • Document uploads during caregiver screening and verification.
  • Official state and territory verification registers (e.g., online government WWCC verification portals) used to confirm caregiver validity prior to platform approval.

4. Purpose of Collection and Use

Lauryn & Littles collects and uses your personal details for genuine business and child-welfare purposes, including:

  • Assessing and processing your childcare requests and dispatching accurate quotes.
  • Connecting compatible families with suitable, verified local babysitters.
  • Verifying caregiver credentials, identity, and working-with-children legal eligibility.
  • Facilitating direct communication between matched parties for booking logistics.
  • Providing customer support, resolving scheduling conflicts, and collecting service feedback.
  • Fulfilling legal and regulatory child protection obligations under Australian state and federal laws.

5. Disclosure of Personal Information

We treat your information with high confidentiality. We only disclose personal information under the following circumstances:

  • Between Matched Families & Sitters: Once a booking enquiry is confirmed, essential details (such as the family’s suburb/address, contact phone number, children’s names/ages, and the sitter’s name, contact number, and verification status) are exchanged between the parties to enable safe service delivery.
  • Service Providers: Secure technology platforms assisting our business operations (e.g., encrypted cloud databases, form processing tools, SMS dispatch gateways, and accounting providers).
  • Emergency Situations: In an urgent medical emergency, relevant health or location details may be immediately disclosed to emergency first responders (Ambulance, 000, or medical professionals).
  • Legal & Mandatory Reporting: Where required or authorized by Australian law, court order, or mandatory child welfare reporting guidelines.

We do not disclose personal information to overseas entities unless using reputable, enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure adhering to strict security and privacy standards comparable to Australian laws.

6. Data Security and Storage

We take reasonable commercial and technical measures to protect all stored information from misuse, loss, unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure. These measures include SSL/TLS encryption, restricted access permissions, and secure cloud storage protocols.

When personal information is no longer needed for any authorized purpose or legal requirement, we take reasonable steps to securely destroy or permanently de-identify the data.

7. Working with Children Checks (WWCC) & Safety Compliance

In compliance with Australian child protection legislation (including NSW Child Protection Working with Children Act, Victorian Worker Screening Act, and equivalent state statutes), Lauryn & Littles maintains records verifying that active caregivers hold a verified Working with Children Check or clearance.

Parents are always encouraged to sight a caregiver’s physical or digital WWCC card and photographic ID upon their arrival at the family residence.

8. Your Rights: Access and Correction

Under the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs 12 and 13), you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request corrections or updates if any information is inaccurate, out-of-date, or incomplete.
  • Request deletion of your profile or records, subject to statutory record-keeping requirements under Australian law.

To submit an access or correction request, please contact our Privacy Officer at example@laurynandlittles.com.au. We will respond within 30 business days.

9. Notifiable Data Breaches (NDB) Scheme

In the unlikely event of an unauthorized data access, disclosure, or loss likely to cause serious harm, Lauryn & Littles complies with the mandatory requirements of the Australian Notifiable Data Breaches (NDB) scheme under Part IIIC of the Privacy Act 1988. We will promptly notify affected individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

10. Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use standard analytics cookies and browser cache to optimize site performance, remember form selections, and analyze website traffic. Cookies do not contain sensitive child or medical information and can be disabled through your browser settings at any time.
