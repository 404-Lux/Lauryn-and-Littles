1. Nature of the Introductory Service

Lauryn & Littles operates as a boutique introductory agency and matchmaking service. Our role is to connect parents and legal guardians ("Families") seeking trusted last-minute childcare with independent babysitters, nannies, and child carers ("Sitters" or "Caregivers").

Lauryn & Littles coordinates introductions, interviews candidates, reviews experience, and checks documentation. Sitters introduced through the platform are engaged by Families as self-employed independent contractors, and not as employees, workers, partners, or agents of Lauryn & Littles. The contract for childcare services is entered into directly between the Family and the Sitter.

2. Vetting, DBS Checks & Safeguarding Standards

Child safety and trust are the foundation of our community. Lauryn & Littles observes dedicated screening procedures prior to introducing any caregiver:

  • Personal Interview & Evaluation: Every candidate undergoes a personal spoken interview with Lauryn or our senior team to assess communication, experience, and suitability.
  • DBS Criminal Record Screening: Caregivers are required to hold or provide evidence of a valid Enhanced Disclosure and Barring Service (DBS) check (or be verified via the DBS Update Service).
  • Identity & Right to Work: Caregivers must submit valid government-issued photographic identification (e.g. UK Passport or Driving Licence) and proof of right to work in the UK.
  • References & Qualifications: Where applicable, caregivers provide independent professional or personal childcare references and evidence of relevant childcare/paediatric first aid training.
  • Parent Sight Right: Families are actively encouraged to sight the caregiver’s physical photo ID upon their arrival at the home.

3. Enquiries, Quotes & Introductions

When submitting a childcare enquiry through our platform, Families agree to provide accurate details regarding required dates, start and end times, location (town & suburb in Winchester / Hampshire), number and ages of children, and any specific care instructions.

  • Quotes: Quotes dispatched by Lauryn & Littles reflect the requested hours, date, number of children, and care requirements.
  • Minimum Booking Duration: To ensure travel viability for caregivers, bookings generally observe a standard minimum duration of three (3) consecutive hours, unless otherwise agreed.
  • Confirmation: An introduction is finalized once the Family confirms the quote and agrees to the match.

4. Payments and Rates

All rates are quoted in British Pounds (£ GBP) and are transparently communicated prior to confirmation.

  • Payment terms for the booking or introductory coordination must be settled as agreed upon confirmation.
  • If a booking extends past the scheduled finish time, overtime is calculated based on the agreed hourly rate in 15-minute increments.
  • For late-night bookings concluding after 11:00 PM where public transport is limited, a reasonable travel contribution or rideshare provision may be applicable as agreed.

5. Cancellation and Rescheduling Policy

We understand that family plans change; however, caregivers hold dedicated time for your booking. The following standard cancellation policy applies:

  • More than 24 hours notice: Cancellations made with at least 24 hours advance notice incur no penalty fee.
  • Between 4 and 24 hours notice: Cancellations made within 24 hours of the start time may incur a cancellation fee equivalent to two (2) hours of the agreed rate to compensate the caregiver.
  • Less than 4 hours notice: Cancellations made within 4 hours or upon the sitter's arrival are payable at the full minimum booking fee (3 hours).
  • Sitter Unavailability: In the rare event an introduced Sitter cannot fulfill a booking due to emergency or illness, Lauryn & Littles will make every reasonable effort to arrange an equivalent verified replacement.

6. Family & Parent Responsibilities

By engaging a caregiver introduced by Lauryn & Littles, Families agree to:

  • Provide a safe, hygienic, smoke-free, and respectful environment for the caregiver.
  • Fully disclose any medical conditions, allergies, routines, dietary needs, or behavioral notes prior to the booking start.
  • Maintain an active, reachable UK telephone contact number at all times during the care period.
  • Provide at least one secondary emergency contact who can be reached if the parent is unavailable.
  • Ensure all necessary baby and child essentials (prepared food, formula, bottles, medications, nappies) are accessible and labeled.

7. Medical Emergencies & First Aid
